## 1 / The idea we actually adopted
Plantupia is Green Technology experienced as one connected living system. The web journey connects **place → planting choice → material and design → care and continuity → real enquiry or a published product**. This is a visual/experiential grammar, not a proposed official slogan.

FIELD: architecture, credible photographic sources, light, material, arrival. FOLIO: meaningful project alternatives, source metadata and formats adapted to each project, not interchangeable cards. SYSTEM: continuing care, specifications and illustrated plant ownership/history, with no fabricated telemetry or completed services.

Keep the centered + World Index, direct Shop access, the six existing company chapters, source-led projects and native scroll. No new independent lab, generic navbar, blanket cinematic blur, involuntary Rapid Index or fake growth. Current creative direction is adopted internally; Plantupia has not given final editorial/publication signoff.

## 2 / Complete visitor story for demonstration
1. Home: three independent architectural settings introduce Plantupia; no fictional before/after.
2. Selected Work and Spaces: projects grouped by real site; six environmental contexts and ways to ask about a space.
3. Six project studies: Pixels Mall, Audi Cairo, Villa 7, Mazal, EM Sherif, El Hallab, with real proposal photos, options, source captions and contextual transitions.
4. Studio: Read → Compose → Introduce → Return, a real design method expressed with sourced proposals and explicitly illustrative components.
5. Living Systems: continuing physical care and an *illustrative* digital plant/QR ownership and history timeline. It does not claim live data or verified service outcomes.
6. Shop: design library + read-only reviewed and explicitly published SKUs, if the backend exposes them; product enquiry/app handoff, not a web checkout or stock reservation.
7. Start a Space: project, care or general enquiry, ideally sent to authorized Company Inbox, with a traceable receipt and staff follow-up. Current Preview sending is intentionally disabled.

## 4 / Verified evidence and what it does NOT prove
- Previous whole-site browser audit: 17 routes × two widths = 34 HTTP checks, keyboard/menu/history and no horizontal overflow in tested paths.
- Six case pages: 6 × mobile/desktop = 12 source-lead and next-case image checks; slider and options preserved.
- Pixels P08/P11 material handoff: 4 combinations of size and reduced motion; direction reversibility, rapid tapping and keyboard were tested.
- Latest CSS-native material scroll pass: 6 representative routes × 2 widths × 2 motion preferences = 24 test cases. In the tested browser the 470px wheel scroll stayed 470px and reduced motion removed the visual animation. These tests do NOT establish actual-device 60 FPS or full accessibility compliance.
- Latest protected preview: https://plantupia-public-preview-du00ehney-omarkhair70-droids-projects.vercel.app — Vercel READY, not public-release approval.
- Current public alias remains on an older build. Backend/Android master QA PR #253 and public commerce PR #325 are Draft, not confirmed production deployment. A successful Next.js build cannot prove customer orders or staff inbox work.

## 5 / Decisions for Ahmed and Plantupia
1. Confirm which source PDFs, client names/logos, proposal photos, catalogue views and final copy may be **publicly published**, with evidence and responsible approver.
2. Confirm which examples were genuinely installed, and which remain proposals or design alternatives. Approve service scope and any care/warranty or technology claims.
3. Approve company identity, public contact details, language policy (current web is English-first), About story and final title/tagline.
4. Confirm Shop release 1: published product catalogue + enquiry/authenticated app handoff versus a newly scoped web checkout. Do not conflate the two.
5. Designate who publishes real SKUs and who owns Company Inbox response, storage, staff permission and customer privacy.
6. Identify approved official domain and domain/DNS owner. No DNS changes without explicit approval.

## 6 / Two honest finish lines
**Review-ready for Ahmed:** visual and factual review of all routes and case photos, credible connected tour, documented uncertainties, one revision log and a decision meeting. This can happen in protected Preview before public operations are enabled.
**Public launch-ready:** review accepted; copyright/company-content approval recorded; real catalogue and lead intake connected and operationally verified or intentionally scoped out with honest visible states; domain/metadata/privacy cleared; final accessibility/device/performance checks; exact production-configured staging build retested and promoted by release owner.

## 7 / Execution order; stop doing open-ended experiments
Freeze the creative direction → run one authored page-by-page design/copy signoff → obtain company/content/source approvals → integrate and test actual Commerce + Inbox with operations owner → run real-device accessibility/performance + full end-to-end path review → stage/retest/promote the approved release.

**Do not mark the website finished simply because the Preview is visually attractive or responds HTTP 200.**
