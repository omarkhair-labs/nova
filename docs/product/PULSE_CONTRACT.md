# Pulse foundation contract

- Lifetime: 12 hours, assigned by the server.
- Content: text, photo, or video.
- Audience: Followers or Close Friends.
- Visibility: current follow graph, block policy, active-account policy, and Close Friends membership.
- Existing Nova API/WebSocket behavior: unchanged; Pulse endpoints are additive.
- Account deletion: removes Pulse rows and uploaded Pulse media.
