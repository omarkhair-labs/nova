# Plantupia — Refinement V2 Art Direction
Date: 2026-11-21
Status: implementation direction for refinement of the stable website baseline at 3609967.
This explicitly rejects the full-world overhaul as the wrong method.

## 1. Preserve
Do not restart the website.
Preserve:
- current route architecture and navigation model;
- centered + as the world index;
- direct Shop access;
- current Home sequence and existing section order unless a source-backed reason requires change;
- current sector and enquiry flows;
- live commerce behavior;
- source/provenance boundaries;
- source-specific case-study structures;
- mobile as an authored composition.

The goal is not a new site with old content.
The goal is the existing site with materially stronger art direction, image behavior, motion, typography, project presence and spatial credibility.

## 2. What failed in the rejected direction
The rejected full-world pass changed labels, colors, copy and route framing faster than it changed perception.
It over-unified different pages and promoted experimental taxonomy into public structure.
References were used as justification instead of being translated into a coherent visual/interaction grammar.
Result: large implementation change with small perceived design gain.

## 3. Research transfers
### Green Fortune
Source principle:
Plantwall / Plantwire are not generic decoration. The form is a response to spatial constraints, irrigation, maintenance and architectural conditions.
Transfer:
Technology should appear where the physical planting system appears.
Avoid a detached dashboard aesthetic.
A technical note should explain the spatial consequence: wall, hanging field, vessel, irrigation, maintenance access.

### Planteria
Source principle:
The service begins with site assessment and continues through design, installation and scheduled maintenance. Their lookbook also connects real settings to species and planted objects.
Transfer:
Project and sector pages should expose real site questions, plant/vessel information and care responsibility near the relevant image.
Do not separate inspiration from practical evidence too aggressively.

### Studio Olafur Eliasson / Your uncertain archive
Source principle:
An archive can create new readings through association and proximity, rather than only chronology or categories.
Transfer:
Use associative navigation only as a secondary discovery layer.
Do not replace the clear route tree or force a taxonomy onto every page.

### Vitra Magazine
Source principle:
Products, architecture, culture, authors and stories coexist as different facets of one project without being flattened into one card type.
Transfer:
Plantupia may let project, material, care and product occupy different visual formats while sharing the same typographic and provenance system.

### FRAMA
Source principle:
Commerce and editorial/material culture coexist with direct product access.
Transfer:
The Shop can feel spatial and material without hiding product identity, price, stock or inquiry action.

### Gentle Monster
Source principle:
Campaign worlds can be visually distinct while the commercial path remains explicit.
Transfer:
Allow different project worlds and visual moods.
Do not turn every route into the same dark-green universe.

## 4. Visual grammar
### FIELD
Used for the cinematic entrance and spatial chapters.
- Image is not a wallpaper behind marketing copy.
- Use directional reveal / architectural wipe rather than generic crossfade + zoom.
- Dark grading must protect image material; reduce glossy cinematic effect.
- Add very subtle scan/grain, not a visible filter.
- Navigation behaves like a spatial index, not carousel chrome.
- Remove generic pill and circular-button styling where possible.

### FOLIO
Used for selected work and source-led project material.
- Preserve image aspect ratio whenever the source composition matters.
- Captions sit outside the image rather than being painted over it.
- Project metadata behaves like folio registration: number / project / source / status.
- White/mineral space is active composition, not empty padding.
- Avoid equal card grids and avoid arbitrary collage staggering.

### SYSTEM
Used for technology, care and operational continuity.
- Technical notation is quiet and attached to the relevant object/space.
- Physical and digital evidence remain visually distinct.
- No fake telemetry, live dots, charts or sci-fi UI.
- Interaction should reveal consequence, not merely decorate state.

## 5. Typography
Current Georgia / Arial pairing is retained initially for compatibility, but its use changes:
- fewer giant marketing headlines;
- stronger contrast between editorial serif and technical microcopy;
- shorter line lengths;
- metadata alignment becomes a visible structural device;
- italics are used as semantic emphasis, not decoration everywhere.

Do not add a new webfont merely to create novelty.

## 6. Motion grammar
Allowed:
- directional image wipe;
- masked reveal;
- slow physical parallax of the image only when it preserves legibility;
- caption/metadata registration;
- short continuity transitions between related states.

Rejected:
- repeated scale-on-hover;
- arbitrary fades on every block;
- scroll-jacking;
- cursor tricks;
- 3D without a spatial job;
- motion that does not change understanding.

## 7. First implementation gate
Before touching every page, refine only:
1. Home entrance;
2. Selected Work visual field;
3. World Threshold;
4. Project Archive surface.

Success means:
- the stable IA remains recognizable;
- the visual difference is obvious without reading new copy;
- proposal image treatment is more respectful;
- motion has one recognizable grammar;
- no new explanatory section is required to justify the design.

If this gate is not materially stronger, stop before propagating the direction to Studio, Systems, Shop, About and Contact.
