# Plantupia — Whole Experience Reinvention

Status: implementation in progress; not design acceptance. Single continuation branch: integration/plantupia-unified-living-experience-20261126 (#333).

## Creative authority
Keep Plantupia's identity and meaningful principles from the signature direction, not its old screen compositions. No screen is assumed finished simply because it exists or passes tests. Re-examine each journey from its actual purpose. Preserve existing API authority, session/ownership, private/public boundaries, real plant records, and Android identity. New compositions must not silently drop features, accessibility or failure paths. Original artwork, typography, motion, sound only where meaningful, and device behavior deserve dedicated experimentation. Do not import stock template aesthetics or copy a reference.

## Complete design worklist (all remain open for comprehensive review)
- Arrival, authentication, account recovery, legal: rethink first impression, returning rhythm, keyboard and recovery, without replacing real auth.
- Today: meaningful daily care, actual calendar and evidence, not generic metric dashboard; empty and no-action days matter.
- Spaces, plant collection, creation: place and specimen identity, navigation between real entities, first-plant and read-only cases.
- Plant overview, care, calendar, field guide, history, photo viewer and time lens: authored editorial compositions, readable close-up evidence, private capture, dated comparison, durable care submissions and outcomes.
- Handover: meaningful arrival and ownership, real QR/manual code confirmation, retry and revoked state.
- Ask, team/support, membership: context consent, separate hypotheses and observations, real service entitlement and provider failures.
- Shop, object study, catalog, variant choice, cart, delivery, orders, business inquiry: rethink discovery and actual commercial flows, preserve price/stock/quote/receipt authority.
- Create, Circle, community, posts, replies, people, profiles, media, messages, calls, activity and settings: preserve private/public boundaries and reachable functionality; audit retained legacy UI as well.
- Company B2B/B2C/Care and independent website: separate surface evidence with coherent identity, not a copy of Android.

## Technology must participate in design
Living Lens: private photo-led inspection and real outline tracing now; validated automatic masks/identification later. Separate observations, hypotheses and specialist correction. Living Time: actual dated photo evidence; registration and measured change only with comparable capture. Living Place: actual saved plan/placements now, guided camera tracking and ARCore research next; room editing and spatial browsing designed as tasks, not forms pasted below a hero. Environmental analysis: real direct-ray scenario with inputs and uncertainty; full simulation needs additional validation. Living World: versioned spatial/temporal twin with real plant IDs; 3D and automated scan are open. Do not fake live readings, species, growth, health percentages, environmental lux, or 3D geometry.

## Workflow and definition of done
For each journey: source/reference research; alternate design exploration; native implementation connected to real APIs; runnable exact-head visual evidence; critique and refinement; acceptance under error, permission, keyboard, reduced-motion, large text, RTL and device conditions. Build one combined QA APK and matching authorized test backend. Keep research flags off by default. Continue computer vision evaluation, botanical review, capture and geometry work alongside design. Existing research evidence must not be lost. No production merge, hosted migrations, billing or Play release without explicit review. Report implemented/tested/validated/integrated/blocked separately; no claim that a roadmap or green CI is full design acceptance.
