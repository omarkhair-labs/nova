# Plantupia — Living Architecture / One place, many readings

## Creative thesis
The site should behave like an encounter with architecture, not a sequence of unrelated effects. Darb used the nature of its archive and textile material to shape the experience. Plantupia has a different source of meaning: spatial conditions, living material, human use, and the continuity of care. The site remains English-only and its six existing destinations stay directly reachable.

## Reference research — principles, not templates
- Studio Olafur Eliasson, Riverbed: https://olafureliasson.net/current/2063 — a visitor chooses a path in altered space; movement is part of perception.
- MoMA, Take your time: Olafur Eliasson: https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/31?locale=en — light, material and geometry change the perception of place.
- Cota Studio case study: https://www.devanzire.com/en/projects/cota-studio — architectural drawing is a visual language for a digital experience. Do not reproduce its compositions or interactions.

## Chapters as distinct encounters
1. The threshold — a restrained, photographic encounter with space. Existing independent sources remain identified as references, never an invented before/after.
2. Spaces — six environments, whole imagery, optional field notes that can be carried into an editable project enquiry.
3. The Studio — one registered hypothetical drawing. Read the existing conditions, compose planting, show installation access, then show continuing care. Motion must explain the change in the drawing; the visitor may scroll naturally or choose a reading directly.
4. Living Systems — future exploration of time and care as a material history, not a claim of real plant telemetry.
5. About — real company identity and authorized work; no fabricated project archive.
6. Contact — actual consent-based enquiry to the company inbox; retain direct access and honest submission state.
