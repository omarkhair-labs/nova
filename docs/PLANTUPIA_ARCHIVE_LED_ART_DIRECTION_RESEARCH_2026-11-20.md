# Plantupia / archive-led art direction research
**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Scope:** private web research and visual-direction instrument. No production promotion.

## 1. What the existing Plantupia notes already established
The earlier web research remains the correct baseline:
- Lead with **cinematic architecture / space first**.
- Use **botanical editorial depth** as a supporting reading layer.
- Keep experimental digital-nature/data language separate until the semantics are real.
- The site should behave like an encounter with architecture, not a stack of marketing sections.
- Mobile is an authored composition of its own, not a reduced desktop.
- Images should carry the architectural narrative; interface must not cover or compete with them.
- The centered **+** is an Experience / World Index, not a conventional hamburger.
- Do not fabricate installation evidence, plant growth, sensors or client outcomes.

## 2. What the current source archive actually gives us
The private reviewed asset set contains **96 assets** and several fundamentally different source behaviours:
- Pixels Mall: repeated views of one commercial place / spatial sequence.
- Audi Cairo: interior chapters and planting interventions across distinct zones.
- Mazal: same facade, multiple planting identities.
- EM Sherif / El Hallab: object-like planting alternatives that read as comparative specimens.
- Villa 7: 24 choices / directions — too much evidence to reduce to one cover image.
- Hospitality catalogue: plants, vessels, finishes and spatial settings.

**Finding:** the archive is not one content type. A single universal “case study layout” destroys information that already exists in the source.

## 3. External research — principles, not templates
### Studio Olafur Eliasson / Riverbed
https://olafureliasson.net/artwork/riverbed-2014
Movement through the work changes perception. The visitor chooses a path through an altered spatial condition.

**Transfer:** navigation can be an act of reading the place, not a menu placed on top of it.

### Studio Olafur Eliasson / Your uncertain archive
https://olafureliasson.net/artwork/your-uncertain-archive-2010-2014/
The archive is organised around associations and experiences, not only facts, dates or chronology.

**Transfer:** Plantupia can later expose cross-project relations such as ARRIVAL / EDGE / VESSEL / THRESHOLD without pretending those are company-approved taxonomies. Association is a future archive mode, not yet a production navigation system.

### Gentle Monster / Stories and 2026 Collection
https://www.gentlemonster.com/int/en/stories
https://www.gentlemonster.com/int/en/category/collections/2026-collection
Campaigns create distinct narrative worlds while commerce remains direct and legible.

**Transfer:** art direction and shopping do not need to compete. A strong world can still expose the transaction path immediately.

### NYBG / LuEsther T. Mertz Library digital collections
https://mertzdigital.nybg.org/
Botanical art, photographs, rare books and accession records retain collection identity and provenance.

**Transfer:** captions, source context and archive labels can add depth without turning Plantupia into a generic herbarium aesthetic.

### Vitra / Magazine
https://www.vitra.com/en-un/magazine
Products, architecture, culture, exhibitions and editorial stories coexist as facets of one larger project.

**Transfer:** product, project and cultural/editorial material can share a world while keeping their own browsing logic.

## 4. New working grammar — the source decides the behaviour
The Visual Direction Lab is no longer a presentation board. It now tests three actual archive behaviours:

### 01 / FIELD
**Source:** Pixels Mall  
Move through one place as a sequence. Large image, small source note, direct previous/next and image index.

### 02 / CONTACT
**Source:** Mazal  
Hold the architecture relatively constant and change the planting presence. Alternatives stay visible as design evidence instead of disappearing behind a final selection.

### 03 / SPECIMEN
**Source:** Hospitality catalogue + EM Sherif + El Hallab  
Inspect object, plant, vessel and condition. This is closer to a material/specimen drawer than a generic ecommerce grid.

### Future hypothesis / RELATIONS — not implemented
Cross-project associative reading such as:
- ARRIVAL
- EDGE
- VESSEL
- THRESHOLD
- DENSITY / VOID only if the source supports the language.

This should be tested as an archive reading tool, not promoted as Plantupia methodology without company evidence.

## 5. Mobile art direction
Mobile should behave like a **pocket field instrument**:
- image occupies the primary perceptual area;
- mode selection is immediate and thumb-readable;
- notes follow the image rather than cover it;
- archive strip is horizontally explorable;
- no hover dependency;
- no scroll-jacking;
- no mandatory animation;
- no desktop composition squeezed into 390px.

## 6. Kill criteria
Reject a direction if:
- it becomes a generic luxury architecture website;
- huge typography replaces actual visual thinking;
- motion exists without changing understanding;
- the archive is flattened into equal cards;
- commerce is hidden for the sake of art direction;
- mobile feels like a compromised desktop;
- provenance / proposal status becomes ambiguous;
- experimental UI makes the real work harder to see.
