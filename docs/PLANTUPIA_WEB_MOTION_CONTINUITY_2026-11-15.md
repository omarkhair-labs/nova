# Plantupia — Motion and chapter continuity (Preview)
2026-11-15

The opening and six-sector exploration now share a continuous navigation vocabulary. Photography carries the architectural narrative; avoid UI covering the images.

- Opening starts at Space and all studies are directly selectable. Independent editorial photos, not before/after. Remove duplicate top selectors, on-image markers and hold-to-compare. Optional field notes sit below the scene.
- Gateway: one airy image field alongside three direct chapter links. Mouse hover or keyboard focus previews the corresponding photograph; click or tap navigates normally. No second confirmation click.
- Native same-origin cross-document View Transitions in supporting browsers: short exits/arrivals, no JS navigation interception or mandatory loading screens. Reduced motion disables transitions. Ordinary navigation remains functional.
