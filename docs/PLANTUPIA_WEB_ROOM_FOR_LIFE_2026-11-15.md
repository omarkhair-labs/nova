# Room for life

Creative direction and review notes for [issue #301](https://github.com/omarkhair-labs/nova/issues/301). Review starts from the website baseline at `627f483e561ffdf72103f2739c776d23f9c52247`.

## What the earlier work taught us

The live baseline earns attention with a large, atmospheric photograph and preserves a useful connection between space, practice and care. On a 390px screen, the centered plus needs discovery before its purpose is clear. The changing hero photographs compete with the chapter gateway as invitations to explore. Six environment labels, previous/next buttons and a progress rail ask visitors to operate a gallery before they can compare what matters in different places. The strongest part of the studio and aftercare prototypes is their move from appearance to decisions and observations.

The [public foundation atlas](https://github.com/omarkhair70-droid/Plantupia/blob/research/foundation-source-atlas-20261007/docs/README.md) treats relational life, retained history and care as research findings. The [project context](./PLANTUPIA_CONTEXT_INDEX.md) and [conversion contract](./PLANTUPIA_PRODUCT_CONVERSION.md) define the selected direction around built spaces, installation and continuing care. The early layout, carousel, speculative products and technical mechanisms were experiments. The website uses the research to explain a relationship between the plant, the place and its caretaker. It makes no commercial claims about future products or measured outcomes.

## The authored experience

The first view pairs a quiet limestone courtyard with a clear account of Plantupia’s work and a visible route into Spaces. The rest of the site moves from the scale of architecture to the scale of a leaf. Warm paper, deep green, restrained lichen accents and set-back captions hold different page compositions together. Playfair Display and Inter follow the public typography direction. The sprout and wordmark are review artwork pending the company’s approved assets.

A cinematic reveal would need credible same-view artwork and would make the first decision about operating the hero. An image-led portfolio would place too much weight on an archive awaiting approved evidence. A simulated records interface would pull focus away from the people doing the work. The implemented direction keeps a lasting image at the entrance, puts the interpretive interaction in Approach and gives aftercare a continuous reading order. Native links and radio inputs handle navigation and selection. Motion shortens route boundaries and directs attention to an actual point in the image. Reduced motion presents the changes immediately.

The first visual review checked the entrance, the directory, the courtyard study, aftercare and the brief together. The refinement made the opening service sentence more concrete, increased caption legibility, removed the duplicate accessible text in space links and made the drafted message visibly separate from clipboard access. The contact composer asks for a note and, where useful, a space type and city. Topics retain independent message text while the page is open.

## Asset ledger

The asset directory contains two WebP widths per image. There are no runtime photo or font requests to outside services.
