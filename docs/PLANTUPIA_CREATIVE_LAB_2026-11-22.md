## Why this exists
The full-world overhaul and later cosmetic refinement were rejected as insufficient:
surface changes, copy and transition swaps did not change the visitor's perception.
This laboratory keeps the existing site and production untouched, but allows fundamentally different spatial readings of the same supplied photographic archive.

## Direction 01 — LUMEN (light / spatial field)
Palette: mineral grey-green, sunlit ivory, light olive, deep forest.
Compositional rule: a full architectural scene and an off-axis editorial composition beneath it.
Motion rule: the light follows the pointer with easing; a different *source* photograph becomes the next space without claiming continuity between different sites.
Interactions: previous/next source frame; move the pointer; descend into two large source-respecting project images; enter care and commerce.
Primary question: can the visitor experience Plantupia as a relationship between architecture and sunlight before reading a paragraph?
Kill if the experience simply becomes a dark hero with too much typography.

## Direction 02 — STRATA (same frame / material tension)
Palette: chalk, warm clay, oxidized copper and walnut.
Compositional rule: the fixed architecture remains visible while planting choices are physically compared.
Motion rule: a user-controlled vertical seam with native range input; a materially different catalogue reading repositions the visual story.
Interactions: Mazal Option 01 vs Option 05 comparison; setting/object/material selectors.
Primary question: can *choice itself* be the visual language of the website?
Kill if the seam suggests an installed before/after; it must be explicitly labeled alternative proposals.

## Direction 03 — AFTERIMAGE (return / continuity)
Palette: midnight petrol, moss grey, cool silver and pale dawn mineral.
Compositional rule: a dark, quiet place opens to a daylight editorial evidence surface; return and care are visibly connected.
Motion rule: three source-grounded editorial moments, with clear distinction between design images and actual care records.
Interactions: READ / COMPOSE / RETURN scene reader; enter Living Systems.
Primary question: can the continuing system feel as spatial as the installation itself without a fake dashboard?
Kill if it implies sensor feeds, exact biological change or verified installed projects.

## Explicit boundaries
- This is a creative lab, not a production design or approved brand identity.
- No changes to Home / Shop / Spaces / Contact routes on the base branch.
- No production or public promotion of proposal imagery.
- No image is reused as invented proof of actual project completion.
- No fake telemetry, fabricated growth history, new client claims or sensor dashboard.
- No WebGL merely to impersonate another studio.
- Motion and compare controls are progressive enhancement; keyboard and reduced-motion paths are required.

## Compare the work, not the moodboard
Evaluate all three at 390px and 1440px:
1. Difference felt within two seconds, with copy hidden.
2. Photographic integrity and accurate source labels.
3. How the viewer progresses from entrance to project to system/product.
4. Motion timing and ability to reverse/switch without tearing or layout shifts.
5. Contrast/readability, keyboard and reduced-motion.
6. Whether each route would be distinctive without copying an award site.

## Decision rule
Do not automatically combine the strongest visual details from all three.
One coherent direction may be adopted, or all three may be rejected.
Any full-site redesign follows a comparative critique and source-evidence review, never a bulk color/copy replacement.
