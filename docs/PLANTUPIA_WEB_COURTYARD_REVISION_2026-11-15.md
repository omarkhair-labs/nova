# Where life takes root

This is the second creative direction for [issue #301](https://github.com/omarkhair-labs/nova/issues/301) on draft PR #302. The first draft, Room for life, made the site legible and complete. Its warm paper, oversized serif opening and sequence of service sections repeated the pattern the [Living Atlas study](./PLANTUPIA_WEB_LIVING_ATLAS_RESEARCH_AND_INTERACTION_V02_2026-11-13.md) had already identified as too passive.

## Start inside a place

The experience now begins in the shadow of a passage. The visitor can step through the arch, look more closely at the planting, or return with the person who tends it. The three photographs belong to the same imagined courtyard and use its limestone, light and olive as continuous visual anchors. The scale changes from architecture to tree to hand.

The older public site proved that a dark opening, restrained index and distinct chapters could carry an atmosphere. Its three photographs showed independent places. Here the three moments carry a single subject across the experience. From Planting, the same photograph travels into Approach, where a visitor can choose what to inspect. From Care, the returning hand leads into a sequence about the same courtyard. Nobody has to finish the scene to reach a destination.

- [Of the Oak](https://oftheoak.co.uk/) put several ways of noticing around a single living subject. The courtyard keeps that continuity across a first encounter, design reading and aftercare.
- [Penderecki’s Garden](https://www.pendereckisgarden.pl/en) used an index as a way of entering an authored place. Plantupia’s index pairs each direct route with a view of its atmosphere. Keyboard focus and desktop hover preview it. Touch follows the route in a single action.
- [Kaizen](https://www.makemepulse.com/case-study/kaizen) uses different rhythms within one authored world. The entrance behaves like three film frames, the directory shows one environment at a time, the approach shifts attention within a photograph, and the care sequence is made for reading.
- [The previous Plantupia motion study](./PLANTUPIA_WEB_MOTION_CONTINUITY_2026-11-15.md) put navigation on native links and allowed motion to fall away. This pass uses that mechanism to match the same image across two page boundaries. Users who prefer reduced motion get the destination immediately.

## Decisions and limits

The broad cream service grid from the first draft is gone from the entrance and directory. The contact workspace keeps a light surface so the final action feels legible and unhurried. The last layer stays local and copyable. The native index dialog takes focus and returns it on Escape or Close. With JavaScript off, its trigger goes straight to a footer index with the same destinations. Photos, type and scene motion are local to the site.

The two additional images were made with built-in ImageGen using the preceding synthetic courtyard as a visual reference. The threshold brief moved the camera back into a near-black plaster passage, keeping the same bright stone and olive beyond the arch. The return brief moved to a close view of hands turning a silver-leaved olive branch in that same courtyard. Both images use responsive WebP widths and on-site generated-concept labels. The story represents design and care ideas. The project archive awaits approved material.
