## 3. The non-expanding scope register
**Inside integrated Android V1:** truthful place/plant/care/history, selectable private/public creation, real Circle and Nova interpersonal media, error-recoverable native UX, real opt-in AI provider, cohesive English-first presentation, privacy/account controls, and QA signoff evidence.

**Tracked C1, contract-dependent and individually closable:** space archive/zones, members/invites/revocations, plant move/replacement distinction, sourced plant guides and species identification, advanced assistant/photo help and offline sync. These are **not automatically “shipped”** or prerequisites if their company/source/rights inputs do not exist. Each must get one of three final states with a reason: **implemented and verified / explicitly deferred / company or licensing blocked**. Never leave it as an unowned “maybe someday”.

**B1 — separate company-gated milestone:** actual Plantupia-installed offices, client-service ownership, approved inventory/assets, site brief/handover, maintenance SOP, visits, replacement, response expectations and service plans. [Lifecycle map](https://github.com/omarkhair70-droid/Plantupia/blob/research/foundation-source-atlas-20261007/docs/exploration/06-project-lifecycle-map-v0.md) remains a testable *hypothesis*, not a confirmed workforce system. Can only be committed as a separate scoped delivery after real company validation. No fake field teams or SLAs.

**Public creative website — separate deliverable, not silently abandoned:** original product direction calls for an architecture/space-led Plantupia website using rights-cleared actual imagery, credible case studies and a real contact/proposal route. It has its **own implementation/approval/QA milestone** outside this Android PR. The proposed room/photo visualizer needs independent feasibility/right-to-use review; it is not secretly implemented here.

**Excluded from this Native release:** sensors/soil telemetry, automatic watering/prediction, carbon-score claims, fictional customer sites, 3D architecture as required app navigation, complete Flutter or WebView remake, forced AI vision/unsourced diagnoses, iOS. Original Nova backup and production are protected.

## 4. Execution order and anti-infinite-work law
1. **Audit existing source before modifying a screen**: map each gap to the repo code, owner flow, data field, role, history, original research evidence and an executable acceptance case. Never rewrite or start new research merely because a concept reference exists.
2. Close **G1** domain truth/safety issues, then **G2** cohesive native/media paths, **G3** actual AI/privacy, **G4** explicitly included C1, and **G5** real environment/device and final integrated APK. Work may overlap, but **no gate closes from branch CI alone**.
3. Research may reopen only for a *named blocking evidence gap* (e.g. unclear permission workflow, source licensing, actual installation process, visual interaction failure), with a specific question, bounded comparison, and written **accept / reject / defer** outcome. Research does not continuously add unrelated features to V1.
4. Use existing PR #241 and screen ledger for every code slice: Android/Django tests, exact CI SHA, device evidence where applicable and updating status. A failing CI blocks the next unverified claim, not the whole project's definition.
5. At G5 give the owner **one integrated QA APK** with an evidence summary, not a series of approval requests. A review change becomes a finite issue with reproduction and closure criteria; no arbitrary unbounded “Pass 08/09/10” by default.
6. **Stop rule:** engineering V1 is complete when G1–G5 have recorded evidence and the owner reviews the cohesive APK; each remaining C1/B1/website item is separately **verified, deferred with rationale, or explicitly gated**. Production/Play status remains a different release gate G6 requiring permission.
