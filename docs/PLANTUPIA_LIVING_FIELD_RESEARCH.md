# Plantupia Living Systems · field evidence and installation geometry

Engineering and research record, 26 November 2026. Draft for product, botanical and engineering review.

## What someone can do

An owner or carer can open an existing private history record in Android, inspect capture guidance, trace one visible region, and save its shape and pixel measurements against the original event. Multiple traces can describe different leaves, visible damage, a pot or exposed roots. A viewer can inspect existing records. A specialist with both space write access and the explicit Django review permission can record a reasoned correction. Reviewing an observation preserves the original event and previous reviews. The plant's verified identity continues to come from the existing species and handover records.

A space owner or manager can enter measured rectangular-room dimensions, place an existing plant by its offsets from a marked corner, and record a window centre. Members can read the 2D plan and open a plant's existing detail and history from it. Each save writes a dated room revision. Refresh joins positions to current active plants and their latest history, including records created since the last geometry change. A moved plant is filtered out of its previous space. A competing edit receives HTTP 409 and the editor can refresh. The map has one local frame per rectangular space. Alcoves, multiple floors, wall thickness and tracking drift have no representation in this version.

An architect can submit a dated direct-sun scenario to the room API. Inputs include a saved window centre, opening size and height, floor coordinates, true-north orientation, location and offset-qualified times. The output distinguishes a simulated line of sight from measured conditions. Multiple candidate points and seasons can be probed without modifying the installation record. The unified native branch now exposes the scenario in the recorded room view using the same API.

## Evidence behind the model decision

| Question | Experiment | Result and implication |
|---|---|---|
| Can a compact network separate the foreground flower? | Oxford 17 Flower Category Database. Three evenly spaced eligible annotations per populated class group, 48 photographs total. Included palette indices 0/1/3, ignored unknown pixels, confirmed foreground visually. No ground-truth prompts. | Mean per-image IoU 0.871 for U²-NetP and 0.846 for a fixed-frame GrabCut. Worst IoU was below 0.001 for U²-NetP on image 0264 and 0.074 for GrabCut on 0158. Similar averages conceal very different failures on clusters of small flowers. Keep traced regions available. |
| Can we enumerate installed potted plants automatically? | COCO val2017. Seeded 12 images each with zero, one and multiple target annotations. Crowd cases excluded. One-to-one matching at IoU ≥0.5, score threshold 0.35. 48 labelled instances total. | FP32 YOLOX found 19, missed 29 and added 4 false positives. INT8 found 18, missed 30 and added 6. Neither meets the requirements of an installation inventory. A missing detection must leave a known plant in the inventory. |
| Can leaf shape and texture establish species identity? | UCI Leaf features from isolated specimens. Excluded both identifiers from features. Held out six entire species, used 179 known specimens for the reference index and 48 for calibration. Applied distance and nearest/runner-up gates. | Known top-1 was 33/48. The gate retained 36/48 known queries and was correct on 31/36 accepted queries. It also accepted 34/65 specimens from unseen species. Preserve candidate status and require confirmation. The input is a measured feature vector, not a phone photograph. |
| Can visits be compared after camera movement? | ORB static-background registration, robust similarity fit, withheld matches, overlap and photometric checks. Seeded numerical control with a known 8 px horizontal and −5 px vertical displacement. | Recovered inverse translation within 0.6 px in both axes. Textureless comparison abstained. Output is an aligned region pixel difference. Parallax, changing light and nonrigid foliage require repeat visits and specialist region labels. |
| Can photographs support metric geometry? | Perspective floor homography from surveyed controls plus two independent check marks. Known synthetic projective transform, disagreeing reference and out-of-footprint cases. Checked rectified planar region area against a second scale segment. | The ideal floor example recovered its target within 0.00001 m numerical tolerance. A 0.2 m reference discrepancy and extrapolation were rejected. Field accuracy and measurement uncertainty still require surveyed rooms. |
| Can geometry support a daylight decision? | Single aperture ray intersection plus NOAA solar position with explicit coordinates and timezone-aware dates. Equatorial east-window fixture, morning and after sunset, plus front/back and above-window checks. | The test distinguishes a direct ray from a blocked ray. Diffuse sky, curtains, exterior obstructions, glazing, lamps and leaf-level irradiance need additional inputs and reference instruments. |

Sources and reproducible per-sample results are available for the [Oxford data](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/data/flowers/17/), [annotation procedure](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~men/thesis/menilsback_thesis.pdf), [COCO](https://cocodataset.org/#download), [UCI Leaf](https://doi.org/10.24432/C53C78), [flower masks](living-flower-benchmark.json), [COCO boxes](living-detection-benchmark.json) and [leaf retrieval](living-leaf-benchmark.json).

**Timing and cost.** On one local Windows x86-64 CPU with one OpenCV thread, 512 px maximum input on the flower probe gave median 1.17 s for U²-NetP and 1.72 s for GrabCut. Median COCO inference was 0.89 s for the 35.9 MB FP32 export and 1.46 s for the 9.1 MB INT8 export. Timing includes host load and excludes image download, model loading and network service overhead. It establishes neither handset performance nor a cloud price. For budgeting, 10,000 FP32 detections at that median represent about 2.46 CPU-hours of timed inference, before queueing, storage, retries and review. Current product paths call no model provider. A heavier model should run outside the request transaction, with a job key built from source digest, transform, region and weights digest. Cache only within the authorized installation and expiry policy.

## Follow-up measurements on the unified branch

- **Two-point assisted segmentation**: reproduced both Oxford baselines on the same 48 images and exact input hashes. U²-NetP alone reached mean IoU **0.871**, fixed-frame GrabCut **0.846**, and U²-NetP followed by GrabCut with one simulated positive and one negative click **0.911**. The refinement improved 25 images, worsened 18 and tied five. Its worst case fell below 0.001. The two points were generated using annotation interiors, so this measures idealized assistance and cannot establish real operator performance. Run the existing flower benchmark with `--point-prompts`. The selectable on-photo traces in Android remain human-authored.
- **Open-set retrieval**: repeated the exact UCI split. A 50% shrinkage covariance transform fitted on the reference split moved known top-1 from **33/48 to 36/48**. It accepted **33/48** known queries with **29/33** correct, versus **36/48** with **31/36** correct for standardized Euclidean distance. On 65 queries from six completely held-out species, false accepts fell **34 → 27**. Run the leaf benchmark with `--whiten`. This uses measured descriptors of isolated leaves. The results do not justify an in-app identity suggestion.
- **Native capture and inspection**: the care composer accepts a CameraX still through its existing private draft and submit path. Live Y-plane hints are provisional and do not block shutter release. A retained photo can display operator traces with pixel area, traced outline length for new traces, and uncalibrated colour fractions. Older eligible images get bounded read-time inspection without altering the saved event or image.
- **Native room daylight**: the scenario reuses the versioned reported plan, returns a room-clipped direct-sun footprint at the selected plane height, and offers a timeline and alternative sample points. The app reports the result as simulation. Field calibration, measured illuminance, occlusions, diffuse light, glazing and reconstructed geometry remain outside this result.

## Spatial and temporal follow-up

- **Visit alignment** uses four corresponding user-marked stationary references on the two existing private records. The first three fit a 2D similarity (rotation, scale and translation). Duplicate, clustered or collinear marks, high fit residuals, large scale change or a disagreeing fourth point stop the overlay. The fourth mark is not fitted. The resulting opacity blend is a reversible display transform and stores no conclusion. Perspective change, leaf displacement, colour drift and area change still require controlled capture and independent field validation.
- **Dated room layouts** reuse room revisions and plant identities. Earlier layouts show the positions and latest eligible care entry as of the revision, filtered by current scope. A plant transferred out of the room remains excluded.
- **Sparse native point viewer** reads a bounded colour PLY locally from the existing room workspace. The viewport supports rotation, tilt, pinch and button-based zoom. The import neither places the cloud in the reported floor coordinates nor writes a room revision.
- **Actual multi-view probe** used 34 source images (1, 4, …, 100) from the public [PAA Plant Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JackTrum938/PAA-Plant-Dataset), `Cabbage/20260328-21/Camera/images`. PyCOLMAP 4.2.1 extracted CPU SIFT, matched all pairs and reconstructed without the supplied poses or cloud. The largest connected model registered **25/34** cameras, yielding **773** sparse points at **0.77 px** mean reprojection error. **676** passed at least three views and ≤2 px reprojection error for export. The remaining nine views did not register into that model. This is an unscaled reconstruction of one controlled sequence with some background, with no dense mesh, part segmentation or independent geometric validation. Reproduce with `python scripts/living_research/reconstruct_scene.py local-images scene.ply --single-camera`. The source photographs are not checked in.

## Technology and rights decisions

| Track | Alternatives, constraints and next experiment |
|---|---|
| Detection and instance masks | YOLOX gives boxes per candidate. U²-NetP supplies one saliency mask and can merge neighbours. SAM 2 or MobileSAM can refine a tap or box. Neither prompt alone supplies a botanical part label. Grounding DINO can generate text-conditioned candidates. On the same private, held-out rooms, compare missed small leaves, background false positives, overlap, per-part IoU and human correction time. Meta distributes SAM 2 code and checkpoints under Apache 2.0 with component notices. Ultralytics offers AGPL and enterprise terms. Review each exact export and dependency before shipping. [YOLOX export](https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/object_detection_yolox) · [U²-Net](https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net) · [SAM 2](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2) · [MobileSAM](https://github.com/ChaoningZhang/MobileSAM) · [Grounding DINO](https://github.com/IDEA-Research/GroundingDINO) · [Ultralytics licensing](https://www.ultralytics.com/license) |
| Species, multimodal and edge inference | DINOv2 embeddings with reviewed reference specimens can rank neighbours. CLIP-style image/text similarities depend on prompt choice and candidate vocabulary. A Pl@ntNet API trial introduces a credential, usage terms and off-device transfer. Compare all candidates at matched coverage, with look-alikes and unknown taxa, before displaying identity suggestions. LiteRT offers an Android deployment route. Measure delegate support, cold/warm P95, peak memory and energy on supported phones for the chosen export. [DINOv2](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2) · [CLIP](https://github.com/openai/CLIP) · [Pl@ntNet API](https://my.plantnet.org/) · [LiteRT](https://ai.google.dev/edge/litert) |
| Phenotyping and pathology | PlantCV provides established shape and colour workflows. A top-view isolated rosette has different occlusion and scaling errors from a room photograph. A calibrated planar projection can describe visible area, shape and colouring. An annotator must declare which organ is visible. PlantVillage's controlled leaf disease results do not establish indoor ornamental transfer. Retain symptoms, competing explanations, contradictions and specialist rationale separately. A wilted appearance and a wet-substrate report should lead to verifying drainage before a care decision. [PlantCV](https://plantcv.org/) · [phenotyping datasets](https://www.plant-phenotyping.org/datasets-home) · [PlantVillage study](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2016.01419) |
| Capture, visual odometry and reconstruction | ARCore motion tracking, plane hit tests and optional depth could supply poses for supported phones. Record the tracking state, intrinsics, frame ID and depth confidence. Re-anchor after tracking loss. The tested manual floor workflow works without a depth API. COLMAP provides structure from motion and multi-view stereo. Anchor scale and evaluate independent distances. Moving foliage, homogeneous walls, glossy leaves and glass compromise correspondence. Relative monocular depth can guide ordering. Depth Anything V2 licenses vary by checkpoint, with noncommercial terms for larger variants. [ARCore](https://developers.google.com/ar/develop/java/depth/developer-guide) · [COLMAP](https://colmap.github.io/) · [Depth Anything V2](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2) |
| Scene rendering and environmental design | NeRF and 3D Gaussian Splatting can synthesize novel views. Neither gives verified dimensions or enduring plant IDs from rendering quality. The reference 3DGS implementation has a separate research licence. Keep optional meshes, point clouds or views linked to the installation revision. Radiance is a candidate for sky/glazing/material transport and calibrated luminaire photometry when supplied. Evaluate predicted work-plane illuminance across reference locations and seasons. [NeRF](https://www.matthewtancik.com/nerf) · [3DGS](https://github.com/graphdeco-inria/gaussian-splatting) · [Radiance](https://www.radiance-online.org/about) · [NOAA equations](https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/solareqns.PDF) |
| Data and provenance | Oxford's download page supplies research annotations without a blanket commercial image grant. COCO image rights depend on the original image. UCI Leaf specifies CC BY 4.0. Pl@ntNet-300K has long-tailed and ambiguous labels, requiring observation-level attribution and rights checks. Check dataset, code, weights and derivative terms separately. Only numeric results, image identifiers, checksums and code are included here. Observation source, observed time, method and subject follow the distinctions in W3C's observation model. [UCI terms](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/288/leaf) · [COCO terms](https://cocodataset.org/#termsofuse) · [Pl@ntNet-300K](https://github.com/plantnet/PlantNet-300K) · [W3C SSN](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-ssn/) |

## Run the engineering and model probes

Use an isolated Python 3.12 environment. Install the existing backend requirements. Install OpenCV 4.11.0.86 and NumPy for the offline scripts only. Use the real repository Android build, the CI placeholder Firebase config and a disposable local database. Activate `PLANTUPIA_LIVING_SYSTEMS_ENABLED=1` only in an authorized research environment. The default is off. Existing installations require the two reviewed Django migrations before enabling new server code. A missing mobile capability flag hides the new room and inspection actions.

From the repository root, with local paths to the inputs substituted:

```sh
python backend/manage.py check
python backend/manage.py makemigrations --check
python backend/manage.py test plants.test_field_evidence plants.test_living_systems plants.test_vision_evidence
python scripts/living_research/test_geometry.py -v
python scripts/living_research/benchmark_flowers.py 17flowers.tgz trimaps.tgz u2netp.onnx flowers.json
python scripts/living_research/potted_plants.py instances_val2017.json local-photos detection.json fp32.onnx int8.onnx
python scripts/living_research/leaf_retrieval.py leaf.csv leaf-results.json
python scripts/living_research/register_visits.py before.jpg after.jpg static-before.png static-after.png plant-roi.png
python scripts/living_research/floor_plane.py floor-survey.json
```

Get both archives from Oxford's 17-flower project, the `u2netp.onnx` export from rembg release v0.0.0, both November 2022 YOLOX exports from OpenCV Zoo, COCO's official val2017 instances JSON, and the original UCI `leaf.csv`. Hashes are recorded in the experiment files. Downloaded photographs, source archives, weights and credentials stay outside Git. The model scripts neither train on nor query any customer images. ## Field and commercial validation

Use the existing specialist pilot protocol. Obtain installation-specific permission for operational capture, with a separate opt-in for training or external processing. Record allowed uses, withdrawal contact, expiration and deletion across originals, traces, caches and derived models. Select fixtures in private spaces with the client's representative. Exclude identifiable occupants, addresses, reflections and readable screens. Upload only through an authorized space. Retain originals and labels in approved private storage. Check existing media delivery and retention on the actual deployment before enabling a customer pilot.

At each visit record the same plant UUID and view, a whole-plant image, the relevant organ close-up, and an independent reference where physical units are needed. Record who supplied each observation, time, camera, lighting and maintenance since the prior visit. Take an immediate duplicate before moving, then a second capture after repositioning. Independently label organ instances, occlusions and visible symptoms; adjudicate discrepancies. Separate splits by **installation and plant**, with later visits held out. Keep negative rooms, reflective or green surfaces, multiple plants, unfamiliar species and failed captures in the denominator.

For each candidate release, report per-site and per-class recall, precision, mask IoU, count error, accepted accuracy versus coverage, false acceptance of unknowns, and unsupported causal claims per reviewed report. For geometry, report median/P95 absolute errors against separately surveyed lengths and failure rate. For repeated inspections, report displacement residual, colour drift and absolute within-visit difference before interpreting any between-visit change. Log cold/warm latency, memory and cost per successful reviewed assessment. The current data cannot support a probability of plant health, treatment efficacy or a growth forecast.

| Commercial use | Deliverable and measurable operational value | Evidence needed before selling |
|---|---|---|
| Plant assessment and intelligent maintenance | Reviewed, localized symptom record; fewer repeat visits for unusable evidence | Audit unsupported claims and compare time to specialist decision |
| Digital landscape audits and corporate asset management | Verified inventory linked to room position, history and service records | Reconcile counts against a walkthrough and measure missed/duplicate units |
| Installation twins and biophilic planning | Dated placement revisions, floor-space checks and alternative locations | Compare layout dimensions and clearances against survey before installation |
| Environmental design analysis | Time-stamped direct-sun scenarios with declared assumptions | Add material, sky and luminaire inputs, then validate readings at plant height |
| Licensing and integrations | Scoped event, geometry and review APIs with versioned outputs | Contract the rights to imagery and weights, retention, tenant isolation, support and per-site cost |
