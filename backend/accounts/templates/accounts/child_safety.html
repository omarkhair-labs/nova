Child Safety Standards

Effective: 11 August 2026

Nova has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

These standards explain how Nova works to prevent, identify, report, and respond to content or conduct that sexually exploits or endangers children. They apply to all Nova users and all content, messages, profiles, and interactions on the service.

Prohibited content and conduct

Nova prohibits:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing, requesting, storing, or distributing CSAM.
  • Sexualizing, grooming, coercing, extorting, trafficking, or otherwise exploiting a person under 18.
  • Soliciting sexual images, videos, messages, meetings, or services from a person under 18.
  • Using Nova to facilitate or promote child sexual abuse, exploitation, or endangerment.
  • Attempting to evade enforcement related to child safety.

How to report a concern

Nova provides an in-app reporting mechanism. Open the relevant user's profile, use the available Report action, and select the reason that most closely matches the concern. Users can also block an account to stop further interaction.

Child-safety concerns can also be sent to asrkhair9@gmail.com. Include the Nova username and enough information to locate the account or content. Do not download, forward, or attach suspected CSAM to an email.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate law-enforcement authority.

How Nova responds

Nova reviews child-safety reports and may remove content, restrict features, suspend or permanently terminate accounts, preserve relevant information where legally permitted or required, and take other measures needed to protect users and the service.

Reporting to authorities

Nova complies with applicable child-safety laws. When Nova becomes aware of confirmed CSAM or conduct that must be reported, Nova will report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the appropriate regional authority as required by applicable law, and will cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement.

Child safety point of contact

Google Play and other authorized parties may contact Nova about child sexual abuse and exploitation prevention and compliance at asrkhair9@gmail.com.

Updates to these standards

Nova may update these standards as the service, safety practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
