Delete your Nova account

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Nova lets you permanently delete your account from inside the app. If you no longer have access to the app, you can also request deletion by email using the instructions below.

Delete directly in the app

Open Nova and go to Profile → Settings → Security → Delete account. Confirm the request with your current password. The account deletion process starts immediately after successful confirmation.

Request deletion without the app

Send the request from the email address registered to your Nova account. Include your Nova username so we can locate the account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Request account deletion

If the button does not open your email app, send the same request to omar.khair70@gmail.com with the subject Nova account deletion request.

What is deleted

  • Your identifying account profile, including email, username, display name, password access, and profile photo.
  • Your posts and Story media.
  • Your comments, likes, reposts, follows, follow requests, Close Friends relationships, blocks, and push-notification registrations associated with the account.
  • Your active participation in Nova groups is removed according to Nova's group membership rules.

What may remain

Shared message history may remain visible to other conversation participants without your deleted Nova profile identity, so their conversation history is not silently destroyed. Limited moderation, security, legal, or backup records may also be retained where reasonably necessary to prevent abuse, protect users, resolve disputes, or comply with law.

After deletion

A deleted Nova account is deactivated and cannot be used to sign in. If you later want to use Nova again, you will need to create a new account subject to availability of account details and Nova's current rules.

For more information about how Nova handles information, read the Nova Privacy Policy.
