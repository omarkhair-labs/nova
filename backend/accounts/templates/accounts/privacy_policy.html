Privacy Policy

Effective: 11 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nova collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Nova mobile application and related services.

Nova does not sell your personal information. Nova is not currently supported by third-party targeted advertising.

Information you provide

  • Account information: email address, username, display name, password, and profile photo. Passwords are stored using secure password hashing rather than in plain text.
  • Content: posts, Stories, captions, comments, reactions, reposts, profile information, and other content you choose to publish.
  • Social activity: follows, follow requests, Close Friends selections, blocks, and other relationship settings.
  • Messages: direct and group messages and content you share into conversations.
  • Safety information: reports you submit and related moderation details.

Camera, microphone, and calls

Nova may request access to your camera and microphone when you choose features that need them, such as creating media or making a voice or video call. Nova does not intentionally record or store the audio or video content of your calls. Call signaling and session metadata may be processed to establish, operate, secure, and troubleshoot calls. Real-time call media may pass through relay infrastructure when a direct peer connection is unavailable.

Technical information

Nova processes technical information needed to operate the service, such as device push-notification tokens, network requests, timestamps, session information, and security or diagnostic logs. Hosting providers and network infrastructure may also process IP addresses and similar connection information as part of normal service operation and security.

How Nova uses information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To show posts, Stories, profiles, reposts, messages, notifications, and social connections.
  • To provide private-account controls, follow requests, Close Friends, blocking, reporting, and other safety features.
  • To deliver push notifications and connect voice or video calls.
  • To protect Nova against abuse, fraud, spam, unauthorized access, and technical failures.
  • To comply with applicable law and enforce service rules.

Who can see your information

Information you publish may be visible to other Nova users according to the product feature and your privacy settings. Private accounts restrict access to approved followers where applicable. Messages are visible to the participants in the relevant direct or group conversation.

Service providers

Nova uses third-party infrastructure to operate the service, including cloud hosting and storage, push-notification services, and call/network connectivity. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to Nova and are subject to their own security and privacy obligations.

Data retention and account deletion

Nova keeps account information and user content while your account is active or as needed to operate the service. You can delete your account from the app through Settings → Security → Delete account. You can also use Nova's public account-deletion page if you cannot access the app.

When an account is deleted, Nova removes the account's identifying profile information and deletes its profile photo, posts, Stories, comments, likes, reposts, follow relationships, push tokens, and related active social data. Shared message history may remain available to other conversation participants without the deleted account's profile identity. Limited moderation, security, legal, or backup records may be retained where reasonably necessary to protect the service, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or comply with law.

Read the account deletion instructions.

Security

Nova uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including encrypted network transport in production, password hashing, authentication controls, and access restrictions. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Nova provides controls for profile privacy, follow requests, Close Friends, blocking, account security, and account deletion. You can also choose whether to grant camera, microphone, and notification permissions through Android settings.

Changes to this policy

Nova may update this policy as the product, infrastructure, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
