### EM Sherif

### El Hallab terrace

### Audi Cairo

### Villa 7

### Hospitality Greenery Catalogue
35 non-pricing first-pass candidates; 14 wide.
Best role: future Planting / Vessels / Materials library. Contains hospitality scenes, exterior planter studies, isolated planter/plant images and material families. This source should feed the site beyond Case Studies.

### Pricing catalogue
Only 2 large non-pricing candidates survive the first pass.
Best role: finish/specification reference only if visually needed. Do not expose source pricing in the public narrative without explicit approval.

## Editorial pools
- Hero / atmosphere pool: wide Pixels, Villa, Mazal, Audi, selected catalogue scenes.
- Comparison pool: Mazal, EM Sherif, El Hallab terrace, Villa option groups.
- Spatial sequence pool: Pixels, Audi, Villa, expanded Hallab pending confirmation.
- Object/material pool: Hospitality catalogue + clean EM Sherif vessel views.
- Technical/care pool: care programme pages, dimensions, planter/material specification.
- Exclude by default: pricing pages, explicit inspiration/reference images, duplicates, weak decorative logos/covers, unreadable embedded charts.
